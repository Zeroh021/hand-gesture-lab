Step 1 and 2: Create classes and collect data

Hold a Record button while you move your hand slightly. Vary angle, distance, position and lighting. Target: 40 to 60 examples per class.

Step 3 and 4: Test the prediction

The model is trained as soon as it has examples. It compares each new hand with what it learned.

91% confidence is not 100% certainty.

Minimum confidence to trigger an action: 70%

Step 5: From prediction to action

The model predicts. Your IF / THEN rules decide what happens.
